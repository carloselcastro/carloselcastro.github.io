Carlos Eduardo Leal de Castro

Professor at Ibmec São Paulo, where I teach data science, machine learning and AI, and co-founder of the SIGMA Lab (Sistemas Inteligentes, IoT, Geometria, Modelagem e Algoritmos). PhD in Computer Science from IME-USP.

My research is in music information retrieval — drum acoustics, timbre and audio descriptors — and in geometry processing for 3D printing. I am also a bass player and music producer.
