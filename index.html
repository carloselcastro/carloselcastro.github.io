Research

Three lines that keep crossing each other: acoustics and timbre, geometry, and learning systems that recommend or explain.

Drum acoustics and timbre

How do shell wood, thickness, reinforcement rings, hoops and drumheads change the sound of a tom-tom? My doctoral work recorded tom-toms built from nine construction configurations, each under sixteen controlled tuning conditions, and used audio descriptors and timbre spaces to test which construction changes are audible and to describe their mechanical-acoustic signatures.

Thesis (IME-USP) Timbre 2026 paper Three open datasets

Geometry processing for 3D printing

Parts of a model that hang in the air need supports when printed, and supports are waste. Using the normal field of the surface, I look for a global orientation that minimizes overhang before slicing. The work started in the master's dissertation at UFSC with Leonardo Sacht and continues in a journal article now under review.

MSc dissertation (PDF) SIBGRAPI 2019

Learning, inference and recommendation

Causal inference and recommender systems, applied to recommending physical configurations for acoustic drums in the doctoral work and to matching artists with contractors at Groova. In the classroom, the same tools become courses on machine learning, deep learning and applied AI.
